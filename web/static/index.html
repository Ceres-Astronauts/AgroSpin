Welcome to AgroSpin. We help you design crop sequences that are sustainable, economically viable, and agronomically sound for the Villa Mercedes – Justo Daract (San Luis) & Río Cuarto (Córdoba) corridor, combining satellite remote sensing (NASA SMAP, SRTM) with verified ground station observations (REM, SMN, PIHC) and in-situ calibration.

What problem does it solve?

Mitigates risks of seasonal drought, late agronomic frosts, and shallow saline water table rising in the Cuenca del Morro using models validated by INTA and REM.

Multi-Criteria Rubric

Evaluates 5 quantitative dimensions: Soil Water, Climate & Temperature, Production & Sustainability, Crop Health, and Business/Market Feasibility.

Hybrid Calibration

Integrates NASA SMAP L4 surface moisture with local soil probes (10–90 cm), SRTM topography, and indicative FAO-56 crop water demand balance.
